SilenX — Secure, End-to-End Encrypted Messaging & Calls

SilenX is a privacy-first messaging platform for web and Android. Every message — text, images, video, files, voice notes, locations, contacts, polls and events — is encrypted on your device using TweetNaCl X25519 key exchange before it is sent, and decrypted only on the recipient's device. Our servers relay ciphertext and never hold the keys to your conversations.

Voice and video calls run over LiveKit infrastructure with end-to-end encryption enabled, so call media crosses Wi-Fi and mobile networks exclusively as encrypted frames. Conversation session keys rotate automatically every 7 days or 1,000 messages through a double-ephemeral handshake, and versioned public-key fingerprints let your contacts verify your identity across rotations.

SilenX works offline: messages you send without connectivity are stored in an encrypted local queue on your device and delivered exactly once when you reconnect.

  • Direct and group chats with typing indicators, read receipts, replies, reactions, edits and deletions
  • Encrypted one-on-one and group voice/video calls across networks
  • Rich sharing: photos, video, files, voice notes, locations, contacts, polls and events
